Run from the workspace root:

```powershell
node .\prototypes\donbass-art\server.cjs
```

Load the renderer as a classic script, then call:

```js
chibi8SVG({...STUDY_CHARS.mariaBrown, expression:'neutral', pose:'relaxed'}, yawDegrees / 45);
```

The entry point and signed/fractional yaw convention survive the rebuild. The old projected geometry schema does **not**. Passing an old geometry record throws explicitly. `BROWN_MARIA_GEOMETRY` and `TEAL_MARIA_GEOMETRY` contain `schema:'paper-xy-v1'` and eight `views`. Each view is an independent screen-XY drawing: x points right, y down. The labels identify facing, not an anatomical coordinate system. Bounds default to `0 -27 200 271`; nominal feet end at y234 in the common 200x244 body frame. Read returned bounds for custom head sizes.

Each `view` stores flat arrays of x,y pairs for hair, cap, central bang, short lock, long lock, tuft, ear and face. Keep corresponding landmark counts/order when editing intermediate morphs. The right ear and longer left lock have explicit authored locations per direction; no complete image is mirrored. Hidden rear designs and neutral standing body proportions are inferred from incomplete drawings.

Eye entries are `[x,y,halfWidth,height,lidRise]`. Width zero hides that eye. Mouth is `[x,y,halfWidth]`; nose is three x,y points. `body` is `[centreX,wrapHalfWidth,hemHalfWidth,waistHalfWidth]`. Hands and feet have per-view locations. Colours and ink live in `STUDY_CHARS`. Head scales stay at the owner's 100%/100% defaults. A small quadratic corner fillet rounds polygon joins; there are no cylindrical cross-sections, projected 3D head/limb envelopes, gradients or baked shading.

Layer order: feet/legs, resting arms/hands, skirt/folds, wrap, optional pose arms, rear pony/tuft/bob, brown right ear behind skin (teal retains prior skin/ear order), face-clipped eyes/nose/mouth, joined foreground hair. Hair fills omit upper root closure ink. The back principal views have no face. Expressions and crossed/speaking poses remain simplified experiments; the pose arm placement and overlap need a separate aesthetic pass. The courtyard uses camera-facing sprite planes, not a character mesh or walk-cycle rig.

Fractional angles interpolate XY landmarks with solid materials. `tuftInbetweens` adds authored 112.5/337.5-degree hooks to reduce collapsing turns. This is stylised morphing; numerical continuity does not prove convincing animation or absence of self-intersections. Eight principal drawings remain the priority. Inspect small steps before extending/integrating.
