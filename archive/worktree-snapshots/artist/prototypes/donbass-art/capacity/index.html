What this demonstrates.

Most controllable: simple symbols, consistent line weights, flat panels, polygon facets and props made from a few separate pieces.

Needs more authorship: character likeness, expressive anatomy, irregular foliage and complex turning silhouettes. Procedural geometry makes revision precise; it does not decide whether a drawing feels like yours.

These samples are drawn in code. No AI image pixels, copied icon pack, remote fonts or production assets. Chibi studies are preserved separately.
