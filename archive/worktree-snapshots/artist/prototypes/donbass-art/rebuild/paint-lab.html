What a reference can guide

  • Local colours and warm/cool relationships
  • Hair sheen, matte fabric, shiny trim
  • Shadow hue, contrast and edge softness
  • Small-sprite readability and outline colour

For a supplied SVG, I can work with its shapes. For a finished PNG, I can study the palette and build region masks or redraw parts; hidden layers and exact material boundaries need interpretation.

These are authored SVG paint experiments on the current Maria geometry. Light stays relative to the drawing; it is not the game’s world lighting. Print and gouache use vector patterns; “gouache” describes the effect, not a physical paint simulation. No AI image is used as the character.
