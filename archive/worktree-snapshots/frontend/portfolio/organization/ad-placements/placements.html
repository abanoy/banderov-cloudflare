The artist comes first.

Gallery /gallery/

Let the artwork stay the artwork.

Desktop: one ART-01 banner after the complete 14-work wall, before the footer. Avoid side rails alongside portraits.

Mobile: one end-of-gallery banner after the full wall. No inserted tile that resembles a drawing.

Keep filter/shuffle tools, artwork cards, zoom/detail dialog and previous/next controls ad-free. Samples disappear visually while a native dialog is open.

03 / placement audit

YouTube /youtube/

Separate ads from the screens.

Desktop: one TV-01 banner after the uploads shelf and viewing notes. No banners between the three featured players.

Mobile: the same end-of-page position, below the horizontal swipe shelf. No bottom anchor over playback or swipe gestures.

No overlay, pre-roll, countdown or ad inserted into a YouTube player. YouTube may serve its own ads; the site purchase cannot remove those.

Stories /stories/alternate-1404/

Protect the reading surface.

Desktop: optional BOOK-01 only after the entire reader/footer block, 180px from its edge. Not beside or within the paper.

Mobile: no banner in the reader. A later separate book landing page could carry one; this reader already contains the full manuscript.

No ad on page turns, chapter jumps, font/night changes or Continue reading. No auto-refresh when a book page changes.

05 / placement audit

Music /#music

Listen without a competing surface.

Desktop: no dedicated music-unit. The existing compact artwork/music pair stays intact; use the surrounding homepage banner instead.

Mobile: no banner between track transports, spinning discs, seek/volume sliders or tracks.

No audio/video ad formats; no automatic sound. An empty ad must not move a playing track or change its focus.

06 / placement audit

Games /games/*

Play first. Ads outside the game.

Desktop: keep current raw games ad-free. A future separate launch wrapper could have one footer banner 180px below the whole game + controls + help area.

Mobile: no banner during fullscreen/touch play. A content-rich game detail/launch page may use one below its description, safely separated from Play.

No ad interstitial, loading gate, pause overlay, reward imitation, game HUD or launch-button surround. Original Don Bass NoAds build remains unchanged.

Each game, individually

Don Bass Idols: full-screen application and existing NoAds delivery; leave the build and workers.dev root untouched. Seven Sips: full-screen story/reply interface; no inline banner between choices. Hop Hop Helper: canvas plus engine/loading/start controls; no banner on cover or loading screen. Crusaders Environment: landing redirect plus full-screen canvas/editor/battle controls; no unit on the redirect or overlay. A future content-rich launcher would be a separate reviewed page, not a mutation of those builds.

Sizes and loading

Desktop samples reserve a 90px-tall strip, up to 970px wide. Below 800px they reserve a 100px-tall strip that fits the available width. These are layout reservations, not promised Google creative sizes; real responsive units must be tested for their actual rendered height. If an approved format needs more space, move it to a new design rather than squeezing or clipping an ad.

Reserve space only after account eligibility and consent are known, before requesting a unit. Paid game accounts, pending/error states and consent refusal never load the ad SDK. Empty/no-fill placements collapse once without moving the reader's current focus; no timed refresh or page-turn refresh. A real integration needs layout-shift testing with delayed responses and actual creatives.

Excluded layers

No ads in account registration, Yoti verification, checkout, payment return/error pages, private rooms/chat, privacy pages, image files, manuscript downloads, gallery dialogs or video theater. No sticky anchors, side rails, automatic full-page ads or popups in this proposal. No ads on the retained technical mock study.

The approved $5 one-time purchase covers all games hosted here. Whether that purchase also suppresses banners on non-game portfolio/gallery/YouTube pages needs a separate owner decision before activation; no broader benefit is promised by this map.

Policy references and limits

Google recommends at least 150px from gameplay; this study uses 180px for the game wrapper. This is spacing guidance, not a guarantee of approval. Google also warns against proximity to play/navigation/video controls and ads that resemble content. No site-review approval, revenue estimate, real creative or mobile-device acceptance is claimed.
