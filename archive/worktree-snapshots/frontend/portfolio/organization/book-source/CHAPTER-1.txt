4 | 	Now let’s see here…Yeropa, yeropa, yeropa. Ah yes, there is our heroine! From the far eastern side of Yeropa, there lies Transcarpathia Mountain, a land that was vaguely developed in 1404, a time when leaders sent out expeditions in search of wealth and territorial expansion. Unlike in Europe, to my understanding, Yeropa was rather largely developed in the far northern side. Some places are heavily dense, but not spread across. This, however, will soon change as we venture forward. In other words, the northerners wish to contest those of the south, something that has a counterviewpoint to their ideologies.

5 | 	Nevertheless, the mountain stands within the pillars of faith—a mount of faith, if you will. There is a sense of peace and harmony from nature itself. There was no need to worry about what tomorrow would bring, for even the animals and creatures live by the moment, possibly gathering food for their groups. There was no sense of “Who’s better?” or “Who’s richer?” for a lot of these folks in rural areas work in groups with no competition—a sense of synergy. Survival is a must, of course, but in the land of Transcarpathia Mountain, every step was rather a place where one could get something to eat, mainly berries, or items to be used as medicine. For a lot of the folks within, it might as well be used for agriculture! And to them, this was likely the promised land that was once suggested where the mountain of faith has nearly everything that anyone can seek and gather to one’s heart’s content.

Despite no centralized townships or order in the mountain, there is rather a collection of people that come to acquaint one another. The issue? The place is somewhat considered a clash between folks of the north, and those of the south. Though most of the natives come from the south, the era of expeditions led to more Northerners to migrate. In the matter of our heroine’s situation, this was no stranger than handling situations that creatures may cause some minor commotions to others. In fact, there is a sense of maintaining the harmony of faith from within one’s heart.

Anyways, more or less it serves as an institution for those who seek written knowledge, to write scholarly works of magic of their trade, or to simply collaborate. It is within there that a young girl is to be the one to set on a journey. She is in her twenties, has rather long black hair that goes below the pelvis line, with a long bang that lines down near to the nose bridge, a sort of ruby-like iris that shines under the moonlight. Her name is Anastasia Velir, a rather shy though humble person that excels in faith, but is socially awkward. She can understand and sympathize others, though speaks briefly and concisely, almost as if she’s reserved despite her interests in talking. Those that worked with her from the shrine admires her diligence and emotional intelligence whilst being curious about their faith-based science, but never really seem to truly grasp it fully and, instead, she is noted to be always working alone. This itself allows her to concentrate, but at the price of her social skills. Guess you can say that she is socially distant.
