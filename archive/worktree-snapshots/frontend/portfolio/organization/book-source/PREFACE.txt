2 | Dear reader,

3 | What you are about to read is a conflict that will echo for others to deal with as the centuries unfold. It is a tale that parallels your world from a different spelling. For instance, while you may call it ‘Europe,’ we call it ‘Yeropa.’ Yes, in it, we use a more phonetic pronunciation of our tongues, as we logically should. We curse in German, resonate like the Italians, and express our emotions similarly to the Spanish. In other words, in the world of Yeropa, there are no countries, but rather a large group of ethnicities united by their ideologies. Not only that, technology and mysticism, swords and magic, humans and creatures coexist. Here, we start our story in rural areas and slowly progress to a more urban city—Malta.

I do wonder why we are here if we likely know what is to come, but, alas, I have been tasked by the foreign entity to help you understand what Yeropa has to offer in its parallel connection to your world.
