Privacy Policy — Abetka Balloons

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Abetka Balloons is an alphabet-learning game for young children (English, Ukrainian and Russian alphabets), published by Alexander Banderov.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on the device

Game progress (letters learned, stars, candies, toys, the chosen character and language) is saved only in the app's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Internet

The app does not need an internet connection and makes no network requests after installation.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families policy. Because no data is collected, no parental consent is required and there is nothing to delete on request.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
