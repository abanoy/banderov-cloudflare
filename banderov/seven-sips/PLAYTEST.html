Play Seven Sips

Detailed source and verification notes · Portable game ZIP

Start with one conversation

  1. Tap an opening reply. During the week, build each sentence from an opener, answer and closer. Mouse, touch and keys 1–4 choose the displayed blocks.
  2. On short landscape screens, swipe inside the question or assembled reply to read the rest. These reading gestures do not select a reply.
  3. Notice her reaction, preference tags and heart meter. The question finishes typing before the reply timer begins. Each fragment has its own timer: 3.1 seconds on Day 6 and 3.2 on the final date, including the choices appearing. The first exchange gives extra time and protects you from losing hearts.
  4. Open the settings button, or press Escape, to pause. Resume deliberately. Music, sound effects, fast text and rules are available there.

Then follow the whole week

Days 1–6 have three meetings each, with three exchanges per meeting. You choose the last seat each day. From Day 2, girls you do not meet that day lose up to three hearts; repeat meetings keep that day's connection from cooling. Invitations require at least three meetings and reaching the heart line: 80 on your first completed week, 100 afterward. Day 7 starts a fresh date meter: reach 100 by the end of its four exchanges.

If two girls arrive, the Yap Battle asks you to back one up: tap her side, or use ← / → or A / D. A decisive lead can finish before the 12-second limit. Endings offer Play again, the collection, or Title.

From Day 2, Continue resumes at the start of the saved day. It does not preserve a half-built sentence. To check this, pause after entering a later day, reload, and choose Continue. A save-failure notice in settings means progress may be lost when closing; its retry is deliberate.

What needs your judgment

  • Can you comfortably read and choose all three fragments at the original pace? Identify the day and line where reading becomes difficult.
  • Do the preferences and reactions make your next reply feel understandable? Does choosing a repeat meeting feel worthwhile?
  • Do the recovered silhouettes match your intended characters? Is the archived music the right version, and does it sound right?
  • After an ending, is there something you want to try next? Is the collection readable and useful?

Keep the relationship going for a month

On phones, Continue (when a day is saved), Endings and Expanded Mode stay directly visible, as requested. Expanded Mode unlocks after winning a Quick Match date. The opening reply remains playable.

Win a date in Quick Match, choose Expanded Mode and save that winner (up to five relationships). Continue Days 8–28. Each day has a new conversation theme and three untimed exchanges.

Start with 40 coins. Work a three-hour office shift: handle routine tasks, verify uncertain requests using a known contact and report suspicious emails/texts. Six best decisions earn 20 coins; collect earnings to save the pay and game time. Paid shifts draw from eighteen fictional scenarios, with different sets and order across the month. Reloading unfinished work restarts the same shift. Optional Office practice keeps the original six-message lesson separately.

A café date costs 20 coins and can add up to three hearts after a surviving conversation. Hanging out is free. The meetup is at 19:00: accepting a late shift or skipping the meetup costs 12 hearts and advances the day. Ending a day at zero hearts ends the relationship. New months need seven paid café dates and a positive connection through Day 28. With the starting coins, at least five well-handled work shifts fund those dates; free hangouts fill the other days. Your plan shows date progress and warns when the goal is running out of time. Earlier saves keep their original month plan.

Reading and pausing never advance the game clock. Finished shifts and completed days save; reloading cancels unfinished work or restarts an unfinished conversation. Paid shifts can be reviewed before collecting; leaving a shift without collection cancels its pay and time. Your Quick Match checkpoint stays separate.

These starting values are implemented game rules and can be tuned after playtesting. Check whether earning, dating and making time for each other feel worthwhile.
