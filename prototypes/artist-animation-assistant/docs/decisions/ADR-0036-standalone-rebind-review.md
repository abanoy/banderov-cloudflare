# ADR0036 — Standalone correction rebinding and explicit comparison

Status: scoped controls/review verified; full master and artist quality unfinished.

Use retained correction/version plus existing target controls and a declared pool
sample; never mutate live constraints during generation. Prepare all views off Tk.
Label old placement as old marks over a fresh pose, not a recovered old full pose.
Require explicit open and both old/raw comparison modes at the target before
constraints-only acceptance. Keep read-only full/detail/clock views and no completion
window opening. Fresh source/backend checks, independent merge, rejection, receipt
truthfulness, one undo and owned cancellation are mandatory.

Keep every tool accessible through a compact chooser without changing Notebook
indices or the inherited CREDITS implementation. Source-bind all selected helpers.
Hidden own-widget tests cannot certify real artist/device/accessibility quality.
Evidence: EXP0040results/verification and actual private diagnostic.
