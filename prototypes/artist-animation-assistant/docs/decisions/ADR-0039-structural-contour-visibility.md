# ADR-0039: Explicit structural contour/visibility generation

2026-10-29. Adopted as optional bounded research core and editable new-part output,
not as default or general eye/rough-line model. EXP0042showed point fits with uncertain
homology cannot establish useful shape. Use a controlled known-identity aperture
fixture to separate shape change, detail occlusion and editability. Ordered cubic
boundaries and retained clipped detail permit collapse/reopening, not opacity fades.

Three editable layers and explicit source/end-key geometry remain available. Existing
source actors/approvals/corrections are not overwritten; new identities/versioned
outputs refuse collision. The actual project trial inserts an independent synthetic
part in a corner and verifies persistence/undo, not an actual facial repair.

Controlled analytic results justify this limited capability. They do not justify
automatic semantic correspondence, fitting a stylized chevron to an almond, inferred
new surfaces, pencil-medium replacement, defaults or artist-quality acceptance.
Retain procedural/existing/adapted/custom neural options and the full master goal.
Next general contour graph/part identity and selected-host explicit proposal review,
plus actual intended-contour/medium and whole-task effort evidence.
