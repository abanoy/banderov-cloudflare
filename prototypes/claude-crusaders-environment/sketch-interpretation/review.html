Architectural self-review

The alternating left/right terraces capture the drawing's climbing structure. The first deck striping came from coincident solid/deck faces and was corrected. The initial overhead camera cut off the rear tower; its framing is now wider. Door handles now follow the opened leaves. Bridge arches were lowered under their ramps. A numerical walker junction failure was corrected by selecting the nearest route segment.

Remaining mismatch: cliffs and retaining masses are still more planar and regular than the sketch. The rear structures and heights are interpretations rather than measured reconstruction. Trees are provisional; the drawn branches may call for a different form. Teal is a route overlay, not a proposed final material.
