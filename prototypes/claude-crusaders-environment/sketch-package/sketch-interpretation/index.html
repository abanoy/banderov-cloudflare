Interpretation and uncertainties

Three terrace heights are assumed at 4, 10 and 16 metres. The tall right pillar is a gate-side spire; the upper right form is a cylindrical tower. Stair bridges connect the offset wall bands. The rear pointed shape is interpreted as an open shrine portal. Rounded forms in the lower panel are provisional trees around an enclosure, shown separately. Colours, unseen backs, tree species and scale are authored assumptions.
