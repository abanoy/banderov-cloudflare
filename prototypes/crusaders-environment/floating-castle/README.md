## Local 8v8 skirmish and castle architect

Open **Bot Battle**, or index.html?battle=1, then Start. One human and seven allied bots fight eight enemies in one local simulation. WASD/arrows move and steer, left click aims/fires, 1/2/3 select Star lance/Comet fan/Orbit burst, Q/E rotate the camera, right drag orbits, and Space launches from a pod. Touch has movement and hold-to-fire with nearest visible enemy aim. The existing chibi, 45-degree orthographic camera, pixel50, textures and lighting remain. The tactical map lets you inspect each bot's current role, reason and route.

Health is 100, respawn is 4s with a 1.2s spawn shield. A controlled uninterrupted hit sequence takes about 2.6â€“3.0s from first hit to knockout; actual fights vary with range, aim, cover and dodging. Fan pellets share one damage allowance per volley per target. Actual triangle geometry blocks swept shots. Enemy player damage also removes 35% of that amount from the victim team's castle shield, then core. Castles begin with8,000 shield and16,000 core health. The shield-breaking hit is fully absorbed: shield reaches 0% while core remains 100%; subsequent hits reach core.

Standing on supported ground inside a tower's 14m capture radius claims it in 6s (modest assistance from teammates); enemies contest, and enemy ownership must first be neutralized. Owned uncontested towers arm for60s, then launch a strike every12s with1.4s travel:90 damage for each distant outer silo,360 for the central Crown (15m capture radius). Contest pauses charging; loss of50% control discharges it over3s before a fully captured tower restarts. The strike checkbox is a local testing option. These are charged attacks, not continuous passive damage.

Core zero starts a120s evacuation. New tower strikes stop, pending strikes clear, respawns stop, and bots seek checked routes to safe arena ground. Human fire and existing projectiles still work. The warning footprint is calculated from the current castle collision bounds, excluding detached harbors. At expiry, anyone within that castle's horizontal blast circle is knocked out. Living actors outside every blast circle and above -60m receive a 100-point **local survival bonus**. No online reward or account statistic changes. The clock counts upward; there is no automatic six-minute match timeout.

Bots use finite sight, delayed reactions, imperfect leading aim, reachable-objective scoring, teammate distribution, defensive holding, scouting, low-health retreat and supported projectile dodges. Their walking and pod routes use the same 120Hz movement solver as the human. A sampled map of floors/stairs/pads is explored at match start; route edges, connectors and pod arcs are physically checked. A blocked route is invalidated and replanned. Applied castle changes end the match and require rebuilding navigation. Pause and the battle menu freeze match time. This is a bounded prototype, not a claim of human-level tactics or universal arbitrary-layout navigation.
