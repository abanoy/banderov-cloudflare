## Current pod controls

Choose another destination while standing on a glowing pad. The launch lifts the pod 4 m and gives it upward and horizontal momentum. Gravity carries it through an ascent and descent. You can steer immediately throughout both phases. Releasing the controls keeps the current horizontal momentum; it does not hover.

WASD/arrows or the lower-left pad steer relative to the camera. C rises and Z descends; the touch altitude buttons do the same. Space and Ctrl also change altitude when native focused controls do not consume them. Steering changes horizontal velocity to the chosen direction, at up to 85 m/s. Held altitude input sets upward or downward movement at up to 45 m/s, overriding launch ascent or a late descent. Release keeps that vertical momentum and gravity bends the path again.

Land by descending onto an open floor. Walls and keep/silo solid volumes block movement; sideways steering can slide away from a blocked descent. Guide to pad switches to an optional guided arc; movement or altitude input takes over again. Free pilot explicitly leaves Guide while retaining its current trajectory. Return to castle ends flight and returns to the last visited castle. Visiting Silo island does not change that home; landing at Dawn or Dusk does. Falling below -70 m or flying beyond +/-1500 m horizontally triggers the same castle recovery.

P or Pause freezes flight and clears held controls. Resume requires a fresh press. R or Reset returns to Dawn and clears the current trip. Q/E and camera buttons orbit; +/- and wheel zoom. Look and Map hide flight panels while open.
